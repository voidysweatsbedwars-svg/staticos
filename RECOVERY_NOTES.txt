StaticOS files recovered from the browser Network HAR.

This archive contains 221 files whose HTTP responses were captured with status 200
from https://staticos.surge.sh/ during one browser session.

Important limitations:
- This is a network-session capture, not a guaranteed complete source-code archive.
- Responses returned as 304 (Not Modified) had no response body in the HAR and were not reconstructed here.
- Files loaded only when other apps/features are opened may not be included.
- Keep the original ZIP as well and merge these files into it, replacing only matching paths if needed.
